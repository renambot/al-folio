We used the summer to upgrade the Arcade and its servers, for VR and stereo 3D work, for networking, and for AI.

### VR and stereo 3D

- A second NVIDIA RTX 6000 card was added to the VR server in the Arcade, for stereo 3D work.
- An NVIDIA RTX Sync card now synchronizes the two halves of the wall, so they can be driven together for VR work.

### 100G networking

- The switches in the Arcade were upgraded: a 100G core network, and upgrades to the copper research network.
- The two rendering servers in the Arcade are now connected at 100G.
- The two AI servers in the EVL server room got 100G connectivity with ConnectX-6 cards.

### AI agentic workflows

- Three PCs from Prof. Miranda's urban analytics group were deployed in the Arcade server room to run AI agentic workflows.
