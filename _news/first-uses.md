- We are working in improving the audio in the room. We installed a lot of acoutic tiles (4" deep) over most free space on the wall,
and on the floor, where possible. This improved the audio quality for video conferencing a lot.
- We started doing tours and demonstration in the space.
- We are working on stereo 3D rendering, and the results are encouraging with a few tweaks. We got Unity3D (Thanks to Jason Leigh
plugin) [CC_CANOE](https://github.com/doctorspiffy/CC_CANOE). We got a couple of Unreal demos to work (Thanks to V) and a couple of
web based demos (Ryan and Khairi).
- We received 2 "big" rendering servers for the wall, replacing the existing PCs we used so far. Now using RTX Blackwell 6000, exciting.
- It's starting to be a pleasant space to do work.
