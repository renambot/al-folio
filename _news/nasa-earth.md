The Arcade display wall now runs **NASA Earth**, a mission-control style presentation built from NASA's Earth observing missions.
The wall plays a 30-minute loop of near-real-time dashboards, impact stories and science visualizations, made for very large screens.

The content comes from [earth.gov](https://earth.gov/), NASA's Earth Information Center, whose public exhibits are shown at
NASA Headquarters, the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History and the Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex.
Our loop cycles through:

- disaster monitoring and response maps
- models of recent events
- the Image of the Day
- current global temperature and sea level measurements

The software was installed in the Arcade by Brenda Lopez Silva.
